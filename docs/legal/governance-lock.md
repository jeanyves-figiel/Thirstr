# Governance lock: no ads, no data sale

> **DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Claude (AI) draft, 2026-11-21. Not reviewed by a lawyer or notary. Swiss corporate counsel must review before use (#9). See [README](README.md).

Goal: make "no ads, no sale or sharing of personal data" survive a change of founder, investors or owner, so users can trust it for the life of the service (DPIA R13).

## 1. Three layers
| Layer | What | Binds | Can be undone by |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 Articles of association | purpose + prohibition clause in the Swiss company's articles (Statuten) | the company, its board and shareholders | shareholder vote **only with the golden-share holder's consent** (L2) |
| L2 Golden share / steward foundation | one veto share held by an independent Swiss foundation (Stiftung) whose purpose is to protect the lock | the shareholders | nobody: a foundation's purpose can't be changed by its founder (ZGB Art. 86 only by the supervisory authority, narrowly) |
| L3 User promise | same wording in the Terms and the Privacy Policy, as a contractual commitment to every user | the company towards users | changing it needs users' consent; breach = breach of contract + unfair-practice claims |

Recommended vehicle (to confirm): **steward-ownership** model as used by Purpose Foundation-style structures: Thirstr AG or GmbH + "Thirstr Guardian Foundation" holding a veto share with no economic rights. Alternative: lighter "veto share" held by a trusted third party (lawyer) until the foundation exists.

## 2. Draft clause for the articles (L1)
> **Art. X — Protection of users' privacy**
> 1. The company shall not, directly or through any affiliate or acquirer of its business, (a) display or allow third-party advertising in its services; (b) sell, rent, license or otherwise make available personal data of its users to any third party for consideration or for that third party's own purposes; (c) use personal data of its users for advertising, profiling for marketing, or training of models offered to third parties; (d) integrate third-party analytics, advertising or attribution software that receives users' personal data.
> 2. Paragraph 1 does not prevent (a) processors acting on the company's documented instructions solely to provide the service; (b) disclosures required by law or by a binding order of a competent authority; (c) disclosures a user himself initiates (e.g. sharing his own profile link or data export); (d) paid subscriptions, one-time purchases or donations from users.
> 3. Any amendment or repeal of this Article, any merger, demerger, asset transfer or sale of the service, and any change of the company's purpose require the prior written consent of the holder of the privileged voting share (Art. Y). A transfer of the service to an acquirer is only permitted if the acquirer is bound by an identical clause.

## 3. Draft foundation purpose (L2)
> The foundation's purpose is to protect the privacy and safety of the users of the Thirstr service by holding the privileged voting share of Thirstr [AG/GmbH] and exercising it solely to refuse any decision that would weaken Art. X of its articles. The foundation has no economic interest in the company and shall not receive dividends.

Board: 3 members, at least 2 independent of the company (e.g. a privacy lawyer, an LGBTQ+ rights organisation representative). Supervision: Swiss federal or cantonal foundation supervisory authority.

## 4. Draft user promise (L3, Terms + Privacy Policy)
> **Our promise.** Thirstr will never show ads, never sell or share your personal data, and never put third-party trackers in the app. This promise is part of our contract with you and is locked in our company's articles; it can't be changed without the consent of an independent foundation whose only job is to protect it. If Thirstr is ever sold, the buyer must keep the same promise.
