# F-08 Media & private albums

## Rules
- R-08.1 Public photos: see F-06 R-06.3 + F-15 classifier. Stored plaintext, AVIF, max 2048 px, EXIF/GPS stripped on device before upload.
- R-08.2 Chat media: photos (≤ 20 MB), videos (≤ 3 min, ≤ 200 MB, transcoded on device to H.265 1080p), voice notes (≤ 2 min). Encrypted on device (AES-256-GCM, random key), blob uploaded, key sent in MLS message.
- R-08.3 Options per send: view-once, expiry (1 h / 24 h / 7 d / keep), allow save (off by default).
- R-08.4 Private albums: unlimited albums/items; each album has its own key; **grant** = send album key E2EE to a person; **revoke** = rotate key, re-encrypt (lazy), receivers lose access on next sync; grant expiry optional.
- R-08.5 Viewer watermark (default on): invisible per-recipient watermark embedded on device before sending (robust to re-encode), enables leak attribution on report.
- R-08.6 Screenshot: Android FLAG_SECURE on media viewer; iOS detect → system message "X took a screenshot".
- R-08.7 Receiver blur: explicit classifier runs **on receiver device**; if explicit and sender not replied-to (or Settings = blur always) → blurred with "Tap to view".
- R-08.8 CSAM/minor safety: on-device classifier + hash match before **sending**; positive → blocked, user warned; nothing sent to server except a counter (no content). Repeated → account review.
- R-08.9 Album UI (S-41): grid of albums, per-album "Shared with" list, add/revoke, expiry.
- R-08.10 Storage: blobs deleted when all recipients acked + expiry, or 30 d undelivered.
- R-08.11 **Media tray** (S-41, #135, [mockup option A](https://claude.ai/artifact/XRKiLgLwVEyKAgdnYypj5X)): the composer **+** opens a tray in place of the keyboard (no modal, no scrim: the chat stays readable and scrolls). Tray: `bg.raised`, top radius 24, height = last keyboard height (min 300 pt, max 45 % of screen). The + turns into a white × (45° turn, `motion.base`). Closes on ×, after a send, when the text field gets focus, or on back. Segmented **Photos · Albums** on top.
- R-08.12 **Photos tab**: (1) **Sent recently** row (hidden when empty): photos this phone sent in any chat, newest first, 64 pt squares (radius 12), label "Sent recently" + teal "🔒 Only on this phone"; a photo already sent in this chat carries a small "Sent here" tag. (2) **Camera roll** grid, 4 columns, 3 pt gap: first cell = camera (system camera; the photo is sent at once with the bar's options); then the newest photos and videos of the phone's library, paged by 60; a video cell shows ▶ and its length (m:ss) (R-08.17). "Pick from library" sends the picked photos and videos at once. The camera cell takes a photo or records a video (≤ 3 min); recording asks for the microphone the first time (iOS; #231, decided 2026-11-21). The video check (F-18) still records without sound. Label "Camera roll" + teal "Manage access" (iOS limited access: opens the system "selected photos" picker; otherwise app settings). No library access → card "Show your photos here" + Allow (asks once) and "Pick from library" (system picker, needs no access). Web (DEV): only "Pick from library". (3) Bottom bar: ⏱ expiry chip (tap cycles Keep → 1 h → 24 h → 7 d, teal when not Keep), 👁 Once and ⤓ Save chips (teal when on), coral **Send N** (disabled at 0). Selection: up to 10 items across both sections, numbered coral badges in tap order. Options are remembered (last used) on the device; Safe Mode or Settings › Auto-delete force 👁 Once on (chip locked).
- R-08.13 **Send**: each selected item becomes its own media message, in selection order, with the bar's options (R-08.3). Photos are re-encoded on the phone (JPEG 0.85, max 2560 px, no EXIF/GPS, R-08.1); videos see R-08.17. A failed upload shows the item as failed in the chat with "Tap to retry"; the others still go.
- R-08.14 **Sent recently store** (device-only, data-model.md): after each successful photo send the phone keeps the re-encoded photo, encrypted with its own random AES-256-GCM key, as a file in the app's private folder; the index (id, key, size, chats it was sent to, time) lives in the SQLCipher store (`recent.media`). Last 30 photos (oldest file deleted first; 8 on the web DEV build). Videos are not kept. Sending one again uploads a **new** encrypted copy with a new key (never re-uses a blob). Long-press a recent photo → "Remove from Sent recently". Settings › Chats & media › "Clear Sent recently" (shows the count) deletes all; sign-out, account delete and duress wipe delete all. Never in backups in readable form (key only in the device store).
- R-08.15 **Albums tab**: 2-column grid of own albums: cover (first item, decrypted on the phone), name, "N photos", pill PRIVATE (violet) or SHARED · ‹time left› (teal) when shared in this chat; last card dashed "＋ New private album" (name sheet → create → add photos/videos → share sheet; the name keyboard never closes the tray, #222). Tap album → **Share sheet**: "Share ‹album› with ‹name›" (groups: "with this group"), cover strip, "For how long" segmented 1 h · **24 h** · 7 d · Until I stop, violet **Share album**, teal note "He gets the keys end to end. Stop sharing any time: the album is re-locked and he loses access." Already shared here → "Shared until ‹time›" + **Change time** (re-grant) + **Stop sharing** (R-08.4 revoke). Empty album → "Add photos first" (opens the album).
- R-08.16 **Album card in chat** (replaces the text line): 230 pt card; mosaic of the first 3 items (1 large + 2 small, "+N" on the last) decrypted with the granted keys, blurred for receivers under R-08.7; lock + album name; "N photos · ‹time left›" (or "Until they stop"). Receiver: violet **Open album** (S-42 viewer). Sender: "You shared ‹name›", "Until ‹time›", ghost **Stop sharing**. Revoked → "🔒 Album no longer shared"; expired → "🔒 Album access ended".
- R-08.17 **Videos** (chat + private albums, #138): up to **3 min**; recorded with the tray's camera, or picked from the camera roll or "Pick from library" (chat), or "Add photos" (album; photos and videos). iOS: the system picker exports at 1080p HEVC. **Then on the phone, before anything is uploaded**, every metadata box of the MP4/QuickTime file (`udta`, `meta`/`keys` incl. `com.apple.quicktime.location.ISO6709`, `©xyz`, make/model, XMP `uuid`, at file, movie and track level) is overwritten in place by an equally sized zero-filled `free` box (no offset moves, the file still plays) and the creation/modification times in `mvhd`/`tkhd`/`mdhd` are zeroed. A file that is not MP4/QuickTime is refused. **Beta cap 50 MB after export** ("Video too large (max 50 MB). Pick a shorter one.") and 3 min ("Video too long (max 3 min)."); the other picked items still go. Encrypted like photos (one AES-256-GCM key per video, new blob each send); not kept in Sent recently. **Bubble**: dark tile (photo aspect when known) with ▶ and length; nothing is downloaded until tapped (data saving). **Viewer (S-43)**: downloads the ciphertext (cached as ciphertext like photos, except view-once), decrypts into **one temp file in the app cache**, plays it with the native player (controls, no picture-in-picture, no background play), and **deletes the file when the viewer closes**; leftovers from a crash are deleted at the next video and on sign-out/wipe. View-once: tile "👁 View once", marked opened when the viewer closes. Expiry applies like photos. **Albums**: video items show ▶ + length in the grid and in the chat album card mosaic; covers use the first photo (videos are not downloaded for a cover); counts read "N photos · M videos". The album photo pager holds photos only; a video opens the video viewer (owner: with Remove).
- R-08.18 Private album **Open to all friends** (#247): see F-07 R-07.45.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC-08.1 Uploaded public photos contain no EXIF.
- AC-08.2 Revoked album not decryptable by revoked device after next sync (test).
- AC-08.3 View-once media cannot be reopened after close (local file wiped).
- AC-08.4 Sent recently: files on disk are ciphertext only; Clear and sign-out leave no file and no index; re-send uploads a new blob with a new key.
- AC-08.5 Tray: chat messages stay visible while the tray is open; multi-select of 3 sends 3 media messages in tap order with the chosen options.
- AC-08.7 Videos: a phone video carrying location (QuickTime `mdta` keys or `udta/©xyz`), make/model and date is uploaded with none of them, the same length, and still plays (test with real files). >50 MB or >3 min is refused with its message.
- AC-08.8 Video viewer: the decrypted temp file exists only while the viewer is open.
- AC-08.6 Album share from chat: grant with 24 h shows a card with time left on both sides; Stop sharing turns the receiver card into "Album no longer shared" and the items no longer decrypt (AC-08.2).
