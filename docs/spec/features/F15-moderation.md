# F-15 Moderation & anti-bot

## Anti-bot gate
- R-15.1 Verification required to be visible and to initiate (F-03).
- R-15.2 Device attestation on every session; emulator/rooted/jailbroken without attestation → read-only.
- R-15.3 Max 1 active account per verified human; 1 profile per account (F-06 R-06.1).
- R-15.4 Rate limits (api.md). New accounts (< 7 d): halved limits.
- R-15.5 Signals (metadata only): identical first-message hash across ≥ 5 recipients (client sends salted hash of first message, not content), link attempts in first messages, reply ratio < 5 % over 50 sends, 24/7 activity, location velocity, cell-hopping, copy-paste bios (server-side similarity on public bios).
- R-15.6 Score ≥ threshold → **shadow quarantine**: user sees normal UI, outbound taps/messages/visibility delivered to nobody. Review within 24 h.
- R-15.7 Community: "Bot / scam" one-tap report; 3 reports from distinct verified accounts with good reporter score in 24 h → immediate quarantine pending review.
- R-15.8 Creator profile type: accounts promoting paid content must self-declare; undeclared promotion → warning then quarantine. Creators hidden by default in filters.

## Content moderation
- R-15.9 Report flow (S-50): reason (spam/bot, scam/extortion, underage, harassment, non-consensual content, hate, impersonation, other) + optional franked messages/media + note. Block offered automatically.
- R-15.10 Public photo classifier (self-hosted): labels nudity levels; main photo must be "clothed/shirtless"; slots 1–4 allow underwear/suggestive, reject genitals/sex acts; Each photo also gets `main_ok` (strict pass); only those show in Friends and Sport (F-06 R-06.3). Borderline → human queue (SLA 12 h).
- R-15.11 Hash matching (CSAM list) on all public photos; positive → account banned + legal reporting.
- R-15.12 Bio/text: discriminatory phrases → write-time nudge; post-publish flag → hide field + warning.
- R-15.13 Human moderators: closed beta = owner only, EN/FR/DE. Public launch: regional coverage (EN, FR, DE, ES, PT, IT, AR, RU, TR). SLAs: underage/CSAM/violence 1 h, extortion 4 h, others 24 h. Beta compensating control: content auto-hidden on classifier match or ≥ 2 independent reports for severe reasons until reviewed.
- R-15.14 Actions: warn, hide field/photo, quarantine, suspend (1–30 d), ban (account + LSH face bucket, F-03 R-03.6). Every action has reason code + appeal (in-app, 1 appeal, reviewed by different moderator ≤ 72 h).
- R-15.15 Moderator tool: sees only reported content + public profile + metadata signals; no access to chats beyond franked reported messages; all access logged.
- R-15.16 Transparency report quarterly: reports by category, actions, bot-rate per 1k MAU, government requests.

## Hide, block, report everywhere (#111)
Owner feedback 2026-11-21: "moderation - option to hide/block/report". Mockup: [Hide, Block, Report](https://claude.ai/artifact/BcXqbMNne1uWyakLqfC67c) (option A, three tiles).
- R-15.17 **One safety sheet (S-21)** wherever you meet someone: profile ⋯, map pin long-press (R-04.14), grid tile long-press (F-04), 1:1 chat ⋯ (F-07 R-07.9) and Chats inbox long-press. Same three tiles in the same order and place every time: **Hide** (eye-off, `fg.secondary`, "Not shown to you"), **Block** (ban, `state.danger`, "No contact, both ways"), **Report** (flag, `state.warn`, "Tell Thirstr, anonymous"). Screen-specific rows go above (Explore: Open profile, Tap, Message; chat: View profile, Date check-in) or below (profile: Share, Vouch; inbox: Star, Mute, Archive, Delete; chat settings). Footnote: "‹Name› is never told you hid, blocked or reported. Settings › Hidden & blocked". Group chats and gone chats keep their own sheet without the tiles.
- R-15.18 **Hide**: one tap, no confirm. Removes the profile from the viewer's Explore (map pins, avatars, grid, header counts, R-04). Chats keep working. Toast "‹Name› hidden" with **Undo** for 5 s (DELETE /profiles/{id}/hide). Medium haptic.
- R-15.19 **Block**: confirm step in the same sheet: title "Block ‹Name›?", three points (you won't see each other anywhere; chat closes and taps are removed; they aren't told), red Block + Cancel. On success: toast "‹Name› blocked", the person leaves the current list, the chat is archived and a chat or profile screen closes. Not undoable from the toast (Settings only).
- R-15.20 **Report**: closes the sheet and opens S-50 with the context (profile, explore, chat + conversation id for franked messages). S-50 keeps "Also block" on by default (R-15.9).
- R-15.21 **Settings › Safety › Hidden & blocked (S-71)**: row shows hidden + blocked count. Screen: two tabs "Hidden · N" / "Blocked · N", one-line explanation per tab, rows newest first (photo, name, age, "today / N d ago / N w ago"), Unhide / Unblock pill; toast on success. Empty: "Nobody hidden/blocked" + "Hold anyone on the map, grid or in Chats to hide or block them."
- R-15.22 **Discoverability**: the grid shows a teal hint "Hold a tile for Hide, Block, Report" above the tiles until the first long-press on that device.

### Statement of reasons (Art. 17)
- R-15.28 **Notice detail (S-86)** replaces the one-line card on Account status (S-70 › Notices from Thirstr) for every action, incl. a shadow quarantine once a person confirms it or after 24 h, and auto-hides from severe reports (R-15.13). Blocks, top to bottom: header (action colour + icon, "Your photo was removed", date); **What** (scope: which photo/field/Moment/party or the whole account; thumbnail blurred if a photo; duration "until 28 Nov, 14:00" or "permanent"); **Why** (rule name + one-line rule text + link to the Community rule; or the law and country when the ground is legal); **Facts** (moderator's 1–3 line note, required, picked from per-reason templates + free text); **How we decided** (three chips: "Found by: automatic check / report / our team", "Decided by: a person / automatically", "Reviewed by: —"); **What you can do** (Ask for a review · until ‹date + 6 months›; "Independent dispute bodies" link to the EU list; "You can also go to court"). Language = app language.
- R-15.29 Shadow quarantine based only on bot/spam signals (R-15.6) gets no notice while under review (DSA Recital 55, counsel to confirm); a person confirming it creates a normal S-86 notice with reason `spam_bot`.

### Appeals (Art. 20)
- R-15.30 **Ask for a review** (S-87 sheet): "What should we know?" (≤ 1 000), optional "Add a photo or file" (no, out of scope v1), Send. Open **6 months** from the decision, one per decision (409 after); free; also while suspended.
- R-15.31 Review by a person who did not take the decision; beta exception (owner only, D-12): the notice says "Reviewed again by a person". Target 72 h, overdue in A-05 after 48 h.
- R-15.32 **Notifier review**: on a "No action" outcome the notifier can ask for a review the same way (6 months, one per report). Web notifiers: reply to the outcome email quoting the reference (beta; owner handles it in the console); a review page follows with open beta.
- R-15.33 **Decision** updates the same S-86 card with a timeline (Decided · Review asked · Reviewed) and the reviewer's reason. Reversed → content/account restored at once (beta exception: a removed photo is not re-published automatically; the owner re-uploads it), banner "We reversed our decision. Sorry about that." Upheld → out-of-court + court lines stay.

### Misuse, trusted flaggers, records
- R-15.34 Misuse (Art. 23): ≥ 5 reports in 30 d of which ≥ 80 % "No action" and ≥ 3 judged manifestly unfounded by a moderator → warning notice (S-86, reason `report_misuse`); repeat within 90 d → reports from this account are not handled for 30 d (accepted but marked low priority; still checked for Child sexual abuse). Same rule (manifestly illegal content) for posters → suspend after a prior warning.
- R-15.35 Trusted flaggers (Art. 22): `trusted_flagger` flag on web notifier emails/organisations set in A-09; their notices sort first in A-01.
- R-15.36 Data: `reports` gains `illegal bool`, `legal_type`, `country`, `explanation (1 000)`, `notifier_contact_enc` (encrypted, web/opt-in only, deleted with the report), `good_faith bool`, `channel enum(app, web)`, `ref`, `decision`, `decided_at`, `automated bool`, `notifier_review_state`; `moderation_actions` gains `ground enum(tos, law)`, `law_ref`, `facts (300)`, `detected_by enum(auto, report, staff)`, `decided_by enum(person, auto)`, `appeal_until`, `reviewed_by_other bool`. Transparency counts (R-AD.10) read these. API: `GET /me/reports` (outcomes), `POST /me/reports/{id}/review`, `POST /public/notices` (web form), `POST /public/notices/{ref}/review`. Retention as `reports` (1 y; notifier contact deleted with it).

### Hash matching before publish
- R-15.40 **Scope**: every public image before anyone else can see it: profile photos (all 5 slots and the card thumbnail, R-06.3/SR-10) and Moments (F-21). Not chat media, private albums or videos in them: end-to-end encrypted, out of reach by design (F-07, F-08); those rely on reports with franking (R-07.11). Verification selfies are never stored and never matched (F-03).
- R-15.41 **Order**: upload → `check_photo` / Moment publish → **hash match first** → classifier (R-15.10) → publish. A photo is never `ok_main`/`ok_public`/`live` without a hash verdict `no_match` or `unchecked` (R-15.43).
- R-15.42 **Provider is pluggable** (`Thirstr.Moderation.HashMatch` behaviour, contract in [I-04](../integrations/I04-hash-matching.md)): `Noop` (until keys arrive; verdict `unchecked`), `Dev` (DEV/test: keys ending `-hashhit` match), `PhotoDNA` (Microsoft PhotoDNA Cloud Service, EU endpoint if offered). Selected by `HASH_MATCH_PROVIDER` (`noop | dev | photodna`); keys in Fly secrets. Plus an always-on **internal blocklist**: SHA-256 of the exact bytes of every image removed under a CSAM decision (R-15.45), checked before the provider, so an exact re-upload is caught with no vendor call.
- R-15.43 **Verdicts**: `no_match` → continue to classifier. `match` → R-15.44. `unchecked` (Noop) → continue, recorded on the photo (`hash_state`) so a backfill (R-15.46) re-checks it. `error`/timeout (provider down, 3 s budget, 2 retries) → photo stays `pending` with `hash_state = retry`, user sees the normal "Checking…" state, a job retries every 2 min for 30 min, then queues it in A-03 for a person (fail-closed: no public image skips the check because the vendor is down). A Moment is not stored half-published: publish answers 409 `unavailable` ("Couldn't check this photo. Try again in a minute.") and stays unshared for the phone to retry. Alert if > 20 photos in `retry` (SR-04 alerting).
- R-15.44 **On a match**: photo set `rejected` + `review_state = hash_hit`, object moved to the **evidence store** (R-15.47) instead of deleted, never shown anywhere, thumbnail deleted; account **immediately quarantined** (R-15.6, reason `csam_hash`) and signed out of new uploads; case opens in A-12 at severity 0 (top of every queue, SLA 1 h, R-AD.8). The uploader sees the generic "This photo can't be used" (no tip-off, no reason string, R-15.29 logic); no notice until the case is decided. A senior moderator confirms (ban + legal report R-15.48) or marks **false positive** (photo released to the classifier, account released, provider told if its API supports feedback).
- R-15.45 **Classifier/report path to CSAM**: when a person removes any photo, Moment or reported franked media with reason `underage` + flag "sexual content involving a minor", the same flow as R-15.44 runs (evidence store, ban, legal report) and its SHA-256 joins the internal blocklist.
- R-15.46 **Backfill**: when the provider switches from `noop` to a real one, a one-off job re-checks every live public photo and Moment with `hash_state = unchecked`, oldest first, 2 req/s (rate under provider limits), resumable; a match takes the R-15.44 path and hides the photo at once.

### Evidence store and legal reporting
- R-15.47 **Evidence store**: separate bucket `evidence/` (Tigris EU, server-side encryption + per-object AES-256-GCM key wrapped by a KMS-style key only the `legal` role can unwrap), no public URLs, no thumbnails, writes only from the server, reads only through A-12 by `legal`/`senior_mod` with a reason, every read audited (R-AD.4). Stored per case: image bytes, SHA-256, provider match id/list, upload time, uploader account id, public profile snapshot (name, age, bio, photo slots), sign-up time, last sign-in time, and the IP-hash/country already held under SR retention (never raw IPs, we don't keep them). **Retention: 1 year** from the case (built #287: `Evidence.sweep` in ExpirySweep, retention probe `csam_evidence_1y`, Legal hold +1 year in A-12) (in line with the US REPORT Act preservation period for NCMEC reports), extendable per law-enforcement request (legal hold); then deleted by the retention sweep with a probe (SR-08).
- R-15.48 **Legal report** (A-12 › Report): a confirmed case produces a report pack (JSON + human summary) the owner (legal role) files to: **NCMEC CyberTipline** (ESP registration; PhotoDNA terms expect match reporting to NCMEC and/or the authority of our jurisdiction, and Microsoft also sends NCMEC aggregate match counts per subscriber) and the **national authority of the operator**: Switzerland fedpol (cybercrime reporting form), plus the user's country hotline when known from IP country (INHOPE member: e.g. jugendschutz.net DE, Point de Contact FR, IWF UK). Where the content suggests a threat to life or safety, DSA Art. 18 notice to the police of the member state concerned, or Europol. The console records where filed, date, external reference and who filed. v1: manual filing with a pre-filled pack; API filing to NCMEC later (follow-up issue).
- R-15.49 **Legal register (A-08)** links to cases; government requests and preservation orders logged with jurisdiction, deadline and response; counts feed the transparency report (R-15.16, R-AD.10).
- R-15.50 **Lawful basis**: public profile photos and Moments are hosted content, not interpersonal communications, so EU Regulation 2026/1881 (reinstated interim CSAM derogation, to 3 Apr 2028) does not apply and is not needed; detection rests on GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) legitimate interest + Art. 9(2)(f)/(g) where special data is touched, documented in the DPIA (`docs/legal/dpia.md`, #170). Safeguards anyway: hash-only for known CSAM, human confirmation before any report, data minimised, retention limited (R-15.47), users told in the privacy notice. E2EE chats are never scanned (the same regulation excludes E2EE and client-side scanning). Counsel to confirm Swiss duties (StGB Art. 197) and the PhotoDNA data terms.

### Queue, reviewer tools, wellbeing
- R-15.51 **One prioritised queue (A-01)** across reports, photo reviews, hash hits, quarantine reviews and appeals: sort by severity tier (0 hash hit/CSAM · 1 underage/violence/non-consensual · 2 scam/extortion · 3 harassment/hate · 4 spam/impersonation · 5 other), then trusted flagger (R-15.35), then SLA time left. Each row: type, tier, SLA countdown (overdue in red), target (public display name only), count of open items on the same account, auto-hidden chip. Filters by type and tier; counts per tier on top. Keyboard: `j/k` move, `Enter` open.
- R-15.52 **Reviewer tools** (A-02/A-03/A-12): every image starts **blurred (24 px) + grayscale**; controls per image: hold-to-peek (shows while pressed, 3 s max), Reveal (stays until next item, audited), Grayscale on/off, Mute colours, zoom 1×/2×. Videos never autoplay, poster blurred, muted. Images load at most 640 px. Hash-hit images are **never revealed by default**; reveal needs a reason and a second confirm. Text with slurs is shown normally.
- R-15.53 **Wellbeing**: per moderator, time on tiers 0–1 is counted; at 2 h/day a break card, at 4 h/day tiers 0–1 lock until next day (R-AD.11) except for the owner in beta (warning only, D-12). Category opt-outs in A-09. After a tier-0 item, a 60 s pause screen with a link to support resources. Daily stats page for the moderator themself (items, tiers, time).
- R-15.54 **Appeal review (A-05)**: appeals from R-15.30/R-15.32 land in A-01 (tier = original + "appeal" chip), show the original decision, the facts, the statement of reasons as sent and the appeal text; decision Uphold / Reverse with a required reason (≤ 300 chars, goes into the user's S-81 timeline). The person who took the original decision cannot decide the appeal when another moderator exists; in beta (owner only) the console requires a 12 h cooling-off since the original decision and records `reviewed_by_other = false` (R-15.31). SLA 72 h, overdue after 48 h.
- R-15.55 **24/7 coverage**: deferred until 100k MAU (or earlier if the 1 h SLA is missed > 5 % in a month): vetted EU vendor (EU data residency, DPA, wellbeing programme, no access beyond R-AD.6), uses this console with `moderator` role. Until then: R-15.13 auto-hide is the compensating control and push alert to the owner for tier 0–1 items (SR-04 paging).
