# F-20 Travel plans

Issue #188 (owner request 2026-11-21). [Mockup](https://claude.ai/artifact/8i2ktMzTN6yGF8TdQjvCMz). Decision D-20.

## Purpose
Plan a trip, look around the destination before going, and optionally show up there early so people can start chatting. Anyone listed in a place he isn't physically in always carries the reason and dates ("Visiting Berlin · 3–7 Dec", "Arriving in 12 days"). Free (R-17.1).

## Terms
- **Trip**: one destination city (`cities` row) + first and last day (local dates). Owned by the account; never public as a list.
- **Look around**: Explore (map, grid, counts, profiles) computed from the trip city's centre instead of the viewer's own position.
- **Show me there**: opt-in listing of the traveller in the destination as a **visitor**, from a chosen number of days before the first day until the end of the trip.
- **Visitor**: a profile listed in a city because of a shown trip. **Here now** = his own location cell is within the trip city's area (R-20.9).

## Rules

### Trips
- R-20.1 Me › **Trips** (S-72, row "Trips" on Me under *How you show up right now*, value "2 upcoming" / "Plan a trip"). Card per trip, soonest first: plane tile (travel tint), city name (headline), date range ("Wed 3 – Sun 7 Dec", locale format), countdown right ("12 days", "Tomorrow", "Today", "Here now" while it runs), visibility row (sky dot "Shown in Berlin from 26 Nov" · "Shown in Berlin now" · grey dot "Only you · browse only"), buttons **Look around** (sky) and **Edit** (outline). Last: dashed "＋ Add a trip · N of 3 left" (hidden at 3). Teal footer: "Trips show only the city, never where you are now. Each trip deletes itself the day after it ends." Empty state: plane tile, "Plan a trip", "Look around a city before you go, and say hi early if you want.", sky **Add a trip**.
- R-20.2 Trip sheet (S-73, add and edit): **City** field → search sheet (`GET /cities?q=`, ≥ 2 letters, accent-insensitive, biggest cities first, "City, Country"); **Dates**: month calendar, tap first then last day; days before today or more than 180 days ahead disabled; range > 30 days refused with "Trips can be up to 30 days". **Show me in ‹city› before I arrive** switch (default **on** for verified, off and disabled with "Verify to show yourself there" → S-21 for unverified); when on, segmented **14 d · 7 d (default) · 3 d · On arrival** (= from the first day). **Say where I'm from** switch (default off): adds "from ‹home city›" to the label; caption "Off: nobody learns your home city". Live preview "Guys in ‹city› see": own avatar, name, age and the exact label (R-20.6). Sky **Save trip** (add) / **Save** + red **Delete trip** with confirm (edit). Errors inline under the field.
- R-20.3 Limits (server-enforced, 422 `validation` / 429 `rate_limited`): ≤ **3** trips at once; first day ≥ today (viewer's local date, `tz_offset_min`) and ≤ today + **180** days; length **1–30** days; shown early at most **14** days; ≤ **6** trips created per rolling 30 days (deleted ones count); **one** trip shown at a time per day (two shown trips may not overlap in their shown windows: 409 `conflict` "You're already shown in Lisbon then"). Editing the city of a trip counts as a new trip for the 6 / 30 d limit. Showing requires a verified account (403 `not_verified`); a trip saved by an unverified account is browse-only.
- R-20.4 End: a trip is deleted at 12:00 UTC the day after its last day (covers every time zone's evening) by the retention sweep (`ExpirySweep`, every 15 min); it disappears from every surface at that moment. Deleting a trip removes it everywhere at once; chats started meanwhile stay.
- R-20.5 Account paused, quarantined or deleted → trips are not shown (paused/quarantined) or deleted (delete). Unverifying (re-verify expired) stops showing.

### What others see
- R-20.6 **Label** (component `TravelLabel`, `travel` token fill, ink 10.5–12/800, plane glyph, pill): before arrival "Visiting ‹city› · 3–7 Dec" on profile and in the Visiting row; short form "Arriving in 12 days" / "Arriving tomorrow" / "Arriving today" in chat header and inbox row; during the trip "Visiting · until Sun 7 Dec" (short "Visiting"). With *Say where I'm from*: "Visiting Berlin from Paris · 3–7 Dec". Never coloured like Verified (teal) or a mode; always text + icon (never colour alone). Screen reader: "Visiting Berlin, 3 to 7 December, arriving in 12 days".
- R-20.7 **Profile (S-20)** of a visitor seen from his trip city: the distance line is replaced by a reason card (sky 12 % tint, 1.5 pt sky border, radius 18): plane tile, "Visiting Berlin · 3–7 Dec", sub "Arriving in 12 days · not in Berlin yet" (before) or "Until Sun 7 Dec" (during). The card shows only when the viewer's position (or Look-around city) is within the trip city's area; anyone elsewhere sees his normal profile and learns nothing about the trip.
- R-20.8 **Chat (S-41) and inbox (S-40)**: under the name, the short label while the trip is shown and the viewer is in the trip city's area. First time a chat opens with a visitor who is not here yet, one system line: "‹name› is visiting Berlin 3–7 Dec. He's not here yet." Sent only to the viewer's own screen (never into the E2EE thread).
- R-20.9 **Here now**: during the trip dates, a visitor whose own `location_cells.cell_r5` is within 2 rings of the trip city's r5 cell (≈ 20 km) is here. He then also appears in the normal grid and map at his own level (City / Area / Exact) with the short label on his tile ("Visiting"); he leaves the interleaved visitor slots and is ranked like everyone else (he is never listed twice); his tile keeps the sky outline with the label "Here now" and meta "Visiting · until 7 Dec". The server learns this only from the cells it already has; nothing extra is sent. Hidden travellers have no cell and are never listed anywhere (R-05.7).

### Locals' Explore (option B, D-20, picked 2026-11-21)
- R-20.10 **Mixed into the grid, tagged**: visitors listed for the viewer's area (R-20.12) are interleaved into the grid sheet (R-04.5): the first at position 2, then one every 6th tile (positions 2, 8, 14, …), all before the "Further away" section; visitors left over after the last nearby tile follow it. Visitor tile: same card with a 2.5 pt sky outline (inset), the short travel label top-left ("In 12 d", "Tomorrow", "Today", "Here now") and the meta line "Visiting 3–7 Dec" instead of a distance bucket. Order among interleaved visitors: soonest arrival, then most recently active. Header counts: "N around" includes listed visitors; "Active now" title count includes them when online. Sort label "Nearest first" stays (visitors are marked, not ranked by distance).
- R-20.11 **Map**: visitors are never pinned. One sky bubble "N visiting" (stack of up to 3 faces, 22 pt, sky border) at the city centre when it is in view and N ≥ 1; tap → sheet to half and scroll to the first visitor tile. Unverified viewers see the bubble count only, no faces.
- R-20.12 **Who is listed**: trips whose city r5 is within 2 rings of the viewer's own r5 (or of the Look-around city), shown window open (today ≥ first day − shown days, ≤ last day), traveller verified and active, all Explore visibility rules (R-04.9: blocks both ways, hides, `hide_from_unverified`, `hide_from_new`, age range, Safe Mode visibility, `show_in` ∩ selected intents, Filters except distance). Viewer's Filters › **Show visitors** (default on, saved with filters) removes visitor tiles and the bubble when off.
- R-20.13 Card JSON for a visitor (in `GET /explore/grid` items and `GET /explore/visitors`): the normal Card (R-04.5) with `visitor: true`, `distance: null` and `trip: {city, country, first_day, last_day, here: bool, from: string|null}`; never his home cell, other trips or shown-days setting.

### Look around (traveller)
- R-20.14 Entry: trip card **Look around**; Explore floating bar place name → Location sheet (S-30) gains a **Look around** section listing own trips. While looking around: the floating bar shows the trip city + "Trip · 3–7 Dec" (sky) instead of the level label; a sky bar under it "Looking around from afar" + **Back to ‹home city›** (ink pill). Map flies to the trip city centre (zoom 12); Locate me returns home and ends Look around. State is per session (not remembered after the app is killed).
- R-20.15 Every Explore call (`grid`, `map`, `tray`, `summary`, `visitors` (bubble and "See all" data), `GET /profiles/:id`) takes `trip_id`; with it the server uses the trip city's centre (`cities.lat/lng`, r5) as the viewer point. Only the owner's own, unexpired trips are accepted (else 404). The viewer point can't be moved off the centre, so repeated queries give no triangulation beyond the distance buckets from one fixed point (AC-05.1 still holds).
- R-20.16 From afar: distances are buckets from the city centre (sheet sub "From the centre"); Exact pins are not shown (counts as an Area avatar instead); the place layers (R-04.20) work; Tonight, parties and activities are not changed by Look around in this version.
- R-20.17 Locals in a Safe Mode country (F-14) and locals with Settings › Privacy › **Show me to people looking from afar** off (default on, server setting `show_to_remote`) are not returned to Look-around queries at all (heat counts exclude them too). Look around into a Safe Mode country shows a teal note "People here are protected: you'll see them when you're here."

### Abuse & safety
- R-20.18 First contacts from far away: a first message (key-package claim without prior contact, R-07.2) to a profile whose cell r5 is more than 2 rings from the sender's own r5 counts against **20 per 24 h** (`rate_limited`, "You can start 20 chats a day with people far away"). Applies to everyone, not only travellers.
- R-20.19 Trip to a Safe Mode country (country on the F-14 list): *Show me there* defaults off; turning it on needs a second confirm ("Being visible in ‹country› before you arrive can be risky. Show me anyway?"), sent as `safe_mode_ack: true` (else 422). The trip card shows a teal "Safety tips" link (→ F-14 resources).
- R-20.20 Reports on a visitor follow F-15 unchanged. A visitor hidden by moderation is hidden as a visitor too.

## API
See api.md › Travel. `GET/POST /trips`, `PATCH/DELETE /trips/:id`, `GET /explore/visitors`, `trip_id` on Explore and profile reads, `show_to_remote` in `/me/settings`, `GET /cities` ranks by population and matches without accents.

## Data
`trips` (data-model.md): id · account_id · city_id · first_day date · last_day date · show_days smallint null (null = browse only; 0 = from first day; 3 / 7 / 14) · say_from bool · created_at · updated_at. `trip_creates` (account_id, at) for the 6 / 30 d limit (rows older than 30 d swept). `cities` gains `population` and `search_key` (lowercase, accents removed); GeoNames cities ≥ 15 000 inhabitants (CC BY 4.0) plus a short list of smaller travel spots, loaded by migration from `priv/cities/cities.tsv`.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC-20.1 A 4th trip, a trip starting in 181 days, a 31-day trip, show_days 21, or a 7th creation within 30 days → 422 / 429; an unverified account saving show_days → 403 `not_verified`.
- AC-20.2 A shown trip to Berlin lists the traveller in `GET /explore/visitors` for a viewer in Berlin from first day − show_days, never before, with `distance: null` and the `trip` object; not for a viewer in Munich. Filters `visitors=false` removes them.
- AC-20.3 Berlin viewers get the visitor in `GET /explore/grid` at position 2 (then every 6th) with `visitor: true`, never as a map pin; once his own cell is in Berlin's area he is ranked by distance like locals (one tile only), with `trip.here = true`.
- AC-20.4 `trip_id` of another account → 404; with own trip, grid distances are buckets from the city centre and no Exact pin is returned.
- AC-20.5 A local with `show_to_remote = false` or in Safe Mode is absent from Look-around grid, map and counts.
- AC-20.6 `GET /profiles/:id` returns `trip` only when the viewer point is within the trip city's area.
- AC-20.7 The sweep deletes a trip at 12:00 UTC the day after its last day; it is gone from every endpoint.
- AC-20.8 The 21st far first contact within 24 h → 429.
